Who We Are

Rooted in Sri Lanka's Warm Hospitality

Headquartered in Kalutara, Sri Lanka (Palathota Road), Go Ceylon Travels was founded with a singular purpose: to share the spellbinding soul of Sri Lanka with global travelers through the eyes of proud local experts. We are an officially registered and fully bonded Destination Management Company (DMC) licensed by the Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority (SLTDA).

We believe that no two travelers are alike. That is why our itineraries are never off-the-shelf bus tours. From the moment you land at Bandaranaike International Airport, you are warmly welcomed into our family with your own private vehicle, dedicated chauffeur guide, and 24/7 guest support.

Our Mission

To deliver authentic, transformative, and sustainable travel experiences across Sri Lanka. We connect discerning travelers with the island's cultural riches, untouched nature, and welcoming communities while adhering to the highest standards of safety, ethics, and environmental conservation.

Our Vision

To be internationally recognized as Sri Lanka’s most trusted, innovative, and customer-first inbound travel company. We champion responsible tourism that enriches the lives of our travelers and empowers Sri Lankan rural communities and wildlife reserves.
